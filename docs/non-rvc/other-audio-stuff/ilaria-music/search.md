## Search or paste a link

Start with one track you have permission to download. You can search YouTube Music, SoundCloud, Apple Music and Deezer from the app. Paste a track, album or playlist URL to inspect the cover and tracklist. For a first test, select a single track and confirm its title and duration.

## Check the actual audio source

YouTube Music and SoundCloud can provide audio through yt-dlp. Spotify, Apple Music, Amazon Music, Tidal, Deezer and Qobuz supply metadata for this workflow. The app looks for matching audio on YouTube Music. Inspect the match before downloading and edit its URL if it points to a remix, live version or different recording. Change the match individually before queuing a whole album.
